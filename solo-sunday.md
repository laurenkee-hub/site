    <h2 class="mt-0">About the series</h2>
    <p>Solo Sunday is an event series dedicated to solo performance. It gives solo artists a space to perform without the costs of a traditional theater, lowering the barrier to entry for local artists. Shows happen on select Sunday afternoons in an intimate pop-up space at Mrs. Murphy &amp; Sons Irish Bistro.</p>
    <p>The series was founded by <a href="http://www.scottwhitehair.com/">Scott Whitehair</a>, who put on over 45 shows! In February 2026, Scott passed the torch to Lauren, who is honored to produce this series moving forward.</p>
