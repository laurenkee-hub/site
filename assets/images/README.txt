Drop your real images in this folder, e.g.:
  lauren-portrait.jpg
  dealbreaker-production-1.jpg
  dealbreaker-production-2.jpg

Then reference them in pages like:
  <img src="{{ '/assets/images/lauren-portrait.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Lauren Kee">

You can also replace the .portrait <div> placeholders in about.md and
index.md with real <img> tags once you have photos.
